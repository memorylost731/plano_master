

# EU Renovation Law Landscape for PlanO SaaS Platform

## Harmonizing EU Directives (Apply to All 27 Members)

| Directive | Scope | Key Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **EPBD 2024/1275** (recast) | Energy performance | EPC mandatory on sale/rent; MEPS for worst-performing buildings by 2030; renovation passport concept introduced |
| **Construction Products Regulation 305/2011** (under revision) | Product standards | CE marking, DoP for construction products; revision adds digital product passport |
| **Services Directive 2006/123** | Professional services | Freedom to provide services cross-border; but architecture remains heavily regulated nationally |
| **Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83** | B2C contracts | 14-day cooling-off for distance/online contracts; applies to SaaS subscriptions |
| **GDPR 2016/679** | Data protection | Relevant for processing building/owner data, floor plans, addresses |
| **AI Act 2024/1689** | AI systems | AI-generated building plans likely "limited risk" — transparency obligations apply |

## Country-by-Country Comparison by Legal Family

### Continental/Civil Law (21 countries)

#### 1. Permits & Thresholds

| Country | Permit Threshold | Key Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **France** | >20m² new floor area (>40m² in PLU zones); all structural/facade changes | Déclaration préalable for minor works; full permis de construire above threshold |
| **Germany** | Varies by Land (16 Bauordnungen); generally structural changes + >30m² | Genehmigungsfreie Vorhaben (permit-free) list exists per state |
| **Italy** | CILA for minor; SCIA for structural; Permesso di Costruire for major | Post-2020 simplification; interior non-structural = CILA (no permit) |
| **Spain** | Obra mayor (structural) needs licencia; obra menor (cosmetic) = declaración responsable | Varies by Comunidad Autónoma |
| **Netherlands** | Omgevingswet (2024): permit-free expanded; structural/facade still requires omgevingsvergunning | New Environment Act consolidated 26 permits into 1 |
| **Belgium** | Regional: Brussels/Wallonia/Flanders each have own code; ~all structural needs permit | Flanders most digitized |
| **Austria** | Baubewilligung for structural; Bauanzeige for minor works; thresholds vary by Land | 9 different building codes |
| **Portugal** | Licença for structural; Comunicação prévia for minor renovations | Simplex Urbanístico (2023) simplified process |
| **Poland** | Pozwolenie na budowę for structural; zgłoszenie (notification) for minor | Recent digitization push |
| **Romania** | Autorizație de construire for most works; interior cosmetic exempt | |
| **Greece** | Minor Repair Permit vs Building Permit; electronic system (e-Adeies) | Digital submission mandatory since 2020 |
| **Czechia** | New Building Act 2024: digital building permit system rolling out | Major reform in progress |
| **Croatia, Bulgaria, Slovakia, Slovenia, Hungary** | Generally: structural = full permit; cosmetic = notification or exempt | Varying digitization levels |
| **Luxembourg** | Autorisation de construire from commune; all exterior changes | Small country, single regime |
| **Cyprus, Malta** | Full permit for most works beyond cosmetic | Malta: MEPA/Planning Authority strict on heritage |

#### 2. Professional Licensing

| Requirement | Countries |
|---|---|
| **Architect mandatory for permit plans** | France, Italy, Germany (most Länder), Belgium, Luxembourg, Austria, Greece, Cyprus |
| **Engineer can substitute** | Spain (Arquitecto Técnico), Portugal, Poland, Romania, Hungary, Croatia |
| **Relaxed for small renovations** | Netherlands (no title protection but registration expected), Czechia, Bulgaria |

**Key risk for PlanO**: In ~15 EU countries, plans submitted for building permits MUST bear the stamp/signature of a registered architect or engineer. AI-generated plans cannot substitute for this professional sign-off. PlanO should position as a **tool assisting professionals**, not replacing them.

#### 3. Consumer Protection & Warranties

| Country | Warranty Period | Special Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **France** | **10-year décennale** + 2yr bon fonctionnement + 1yr parfait achèvement | Assurance décennale MANDATORY for all builders |
| **Belgium** | 10-year (Artt. 1792/2270 Civil Code) | Similar to France |
| **Spain** | 10yr structural / 3yr habitability / 1yr finish | LOE (Ley de Ordenación de la Edificación) |
| **Italy** | 10yr structural (Art. 1669 Codice Civile) | Applies to major renovations |
| **Germany** | 5yr (BGB) or 4yr (VOB/B) | No decennial but strong Gewährleistung |
| **Netherlands** | 5yr general; 20yr hidden defects | UAV 2012 conditions |
| **Most CEE countries** | 2-5 years depending on work type | Generally shorter than Western EU |

#### 4. VAT on Renovation

| Rate Category | Countries | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| **Reduced rate on renovation** | France (10%), Belgium (6%), Italy (10%), Spain (10%), Portugal (6%), Netherlands (9%), Luxembourg (3%), Poland (8%), Czechia (12%), Ireland (13.5%) | Significant savings vs new build |
| **Standard rate only** | Germany (19%), Austria (20%), Denmark (25%), most Nordic | No reduced rate for renovation labor |
| **Super-reduced** | Luxembourg (3% for housing renovation) — lowest in EU | |

| Country | Program | Benefit |
|---|---|---|

**PlanO opportunity**: Integrating subsidy eligibility calculators per country is a strong product differentiator.

## Heritage & Conservation

All 27 states have protected building registries. Key patterns:
- **Listed buildings**: Permit required for ANY change (including interior) in France, Italy, UK-adjacent Ireland, Germany, Spain
- **Conservation areas**: Facade/material restrictions even for non-listed buildings
- **Buffer zones**: France (500m around monuments), Italy (vincolo paesaggistico)

**PlanO risk**: Must flag heritage status early in user flow — submitting non-compliant plans wastes professional time and damages credibility.

## Key Risks & Required Disclaimers for PlanO SaaS

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **AI-generated plans used for permits without professional sign-off** | Mandatory disclaimer: "Plans generated by PlanO require validation and signature by a locally registered professional before permit submission" |
| **Subsidy advice treated as financial/legal advice** | Disclaimer: "Subsidy information is indicative only; verify with national agency" |
| **Cross-border professional services** | Services Directive allows cross-border practice but national registration often still required; PlanO connects users to LOCAL professionals |
| **Liability for non-compliant designs** | PlanO must NOT position as the "designer of record"; Terms of Service must exclude liability for regulatory compliance |
| **GDPR — building/owner data** | Floor plans + addresses = personal data in context; privacy policy must cover processing basis |
| **AI Act transparency** | If AI generates design suggestions, must disclose AI involvement to end user |

## Strategic Summary for PlanO EU Expansion

**Tier 1 — Launch first** (digital-ready, large markets, reduced VAT): France, Netherlands, Germany, Italy, Spain
**Tier 2 — Fast followers** (strong digital infra): Finland, Denmark, Belgium, Portugal, Greece
**Tier 3 — Opportunistic** (smaller markets or less digital): CEE countries, Cyprus, Malta, Luxembourg

**Biggest product opportunities**: Per-country subsidy calculators, heritage status checking, automatic professional routing based on jurisdiction requirements, and digital permit portal integration where APIs exist.
