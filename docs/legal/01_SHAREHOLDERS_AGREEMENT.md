## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1. "Act" means the Malta Companies Act, Cap 386.

1.2. "Articles" means the Company's Memorandum and Articles of Association.

1.3. "BDO Nominee" means the nominee director appointed by BDO Malta to serve as sole director of the Company.

1.4. "Business" means the development, operation, and commercialisation of the PlanO floor-plan SaaS platform and all related products.

1.5. "Exit Event" means a sale of all or substantially all shares, assets, or a merger resulting in change of control.

1.6. "Fair Market Value" or "FMV" means the value determined by an independent Big Four or equivalent auditor appointed by agreement or, failing agreement, by the Malta Arbitration Centre.

1.7. "Reserved Matters" means the matters listed in Section 8.

1.8. "Valuation Threshold" means a Company valuation of EUR 16,700,000 determined by independent valuation.

## 2. Share Capital and Ownership

2.1. The authorised share capital is EUR [1,200] divided into [1,200] ordinary shares of EUR 1.00 each.

2.2. **Allocation:** Shareholder A holds 720 shares (60%). Shareholder B holds 480 shares (40%).

2.3. All shares carry equal voting rights, equal economic rights, and equal ranking on liquidation. No share class carries preferential rights unless unanimously agreed.

2.4. **Liability limitation:** Each Shareholder's liability is strictly limited to the nominal value of their subscribed shares. No Shareholder shall provide personal guarantees, pledges, or security for Company obligations without their express written consent. The Company shall not request or accept personal guarantees from Shareholders.

## 3. Vesting

3.1. All shares are subject to a 4-year vesting schedule with a 1-year cliff from the date of incorporation.

3.2. **Cliff:** If a Shareholder departs before 12 months from [INCORPORATION DATE], 100% of their shares are unvested and must be transferred to the remaining Shareholder at nominal value (EUR 1.00 per share).

3.3. **Post-cliff vesting:** After the cliff, shares vest monthly in equal instalments over the remaining 36 months (2.78% per month).

3.4. **Acceleration:** 100% of unvested shares vest immediately upon (a) an Exit Event, or (b) termination without cause.

3.5. Vesting applies identically to both Shareholders.

## 4. No Dividend Policy -- Mandatory Reinvestment

4.1. The Shareholders agree that no dividends shall be declared or paid until an Exit Event or until both Shareholders unanimously agree in writing to modify this clause.

4.2. All distributable profits shall be reinvested in the Business.

4.3. This clause survives any change of director and is binding on the Company.

## 5. Anti-Dilution and Pre-Emptive Rights

5.1. **Pre-emptive rights:** Any new shares must first be offered to existing Shareholders pro rata to their holdings, at the same price and terms as offered to any third party.

5.2. **Anti-dilution:** If new shares are issued below FMV, existing Shareholders receive additional shares (weighted-average broad-based anti-dilution) to maintain their economic percentage.

5.3. Neither Shareholder may be diluted below their current percentage without their written consent, except by unanimous agreement.

## 6. Transfer Restrictions

6.1. **Lock-up:** No Shareholder may transfer shares during the vesting period except to a wholly-owned personal holding company (with Shareholder remaining ultimate beneficial owner).

6.2. **Right of First Refusal (ROFR):** A Shareholder wishing to sell must first offer shares to the other Shareholder at the price and terms of any bona fide third-party offer. The non-selling Shareholder has 30 days to accept.

6.3. **Tag-Along:** If Shareholder A receives a bona fide offer for 50% or more of their shares, Shareholder B has the right to sell a proportional number of shares on the same terms.

6.4. **Drag-Along:** If Shareholders holding 75% or more of shares accept a bona fide Exit Event offer at or above FMV, the remaining Shareholders must sell on the same terms.

## 7. Good Leaver / Bad Leaver

7.1. **Good Leaver** (death, disability, termination without cause, mutual agreement): departing Shareholder retains vested shares at FMV. Unvested shares transfer to remaining Shareholder at nominal value.

7.2. **Bad Leaver** (material breach, fraud, criminal conviction, voluntary departure before cliff, competitive activity): ALL shares (vested and unvested) transfer to the remaining Shareholder at the lower of nominal value or FMV.

7.3. Classification disputes resolved per Section 14 (Deadlock Resolution).

## 8. Reserved Matters (Require Shareholder Approval)

8.1. **Simple Majority (>50%):** Approval of annual budgets, appointment of key employees with salary exceeding EUR [50,000], entering contracts exceeding EUR [25,000] annual value.

8.2. **Supermajority (75%):** Amendment of Articles, issuance of new shares, creation of share classes, admission of new shareholders, approval of related-party transactions, change of auditor, change of registered agent, material change to the Business, borrowings exceeding EUR [100,000], granting of security over Company assets, commencement of litigation exceeding EUR [25,000].

8.3. **Unanimous (100%):** Declaration of dividends, modification of the no-dividend policy, voluntary winding-up, change of company name, any personal guarantee by a Shareholder.

8.4. The BDO Nominee shall not act on any Reserved Matter without the requisite Shareholder approval in writing.

## 9. BDO Nominee Director

9.1. The Shareholders agree to appoint and maintain a BDO Malta nominee as sole director of the Company.

9.2. The BDO Nominee acts on the instructions of the Shareholders (by simple majority unless a Reserved Matter applies).

9.3. The BDO Nominee shall not have authority to act on Reserved Matters without written Shareholder approval.

9.4. The Company indemnifies the BDO Nominee per the Director Service Agreement.

9.5. The Shareholders may replace the BDO Nominee by simple majority vote, appointing a replacement nominee from BDO Malta or another licensed fiduciary.

## 10. IP Assignment

10.1. Each Shareholder irrevocably assigns to the Company all intellectual property created for or relating to the Business, in accordance with the IP Assignment Agreement executed contemporaneously.

10.2. Future IP created by either Shareholder in the course of Business activities automatically vests in the Company.

## 11. Non-Compete and Confidentiality

11.1. **Non-compete:** During engagement and for 24 months after cessation, neither Shareholder shall directly or indirectly engage in a competing floor-plan, renovation-estimation, or contractor-SaaS business within the European Union.

11.2. **Confidentiality:** Perpetual obligation. Survives termination of this Agreement.

11.3. **Non-solicitation:** 24 months post-cessation. Covers employees, consultants, and key clients.

## 12. Exit Provisions

12.1. **Trigger:** Either Shareholder may trigger a sale process after: (a) the Company reaches the Valuation Threshold (EUR 16.7M), OR (b) 4 years from incorporation, whichever is earlier.

12.2. **Process:** The triggering Shareholder serves written notice. The Shareholders then have 90 days to jointly appoint an investment bank or broker. If no agreement, the Malta Arbitration Centre appoints one.

12.3. **Minimum price:** No Exit Event shall be completed below FMV unless both Shareholders consent in writing.

12.4. **Clean exit:** No Shareholder shall be required to provide personal guarantees, earnouts tied to personal performance, or non-compete beyond 24 months as a condition of any Exit Event.

12.5. **Distribution:** Exit proceeds distributed pro rata to shareholding after Company debts, taxes, and transaction costs.

## 13. Decision-Making

13.1. **Ordinary decisions:** Simple majority of shares (>50%). Shareholder A holds 60% and can pass ordinary resolutions.

13.2. **Fundamental changes:** 75% supermajority required (see Section 8.2). Neither Shareholder alone can pass these.

13.3. **Protective provisions:** Unanimous consent for matters in Section 8.3 gives Shareholder B effective veto on dividend policy changes and winding-up.

## 14. Deadlock Resolution

14.1. **Negotiation:** 14 days good-faith negotiation between Shareholders.

14.2. **Mediation:** If unresolved, 30 days mediation under the Malta Mediation Centre.

14.3. **Arbitration:** If mediation fails, binding arbitration under the Malta Arbitration Centre, single arbitrator, in English. Award is final and enforceable.

14.4. **Russian Roulette (last resort):** If arbitration is declined by both parties, either Shareholder may serve a buy-sell notice specifying a price per share. The recipient must either buy at that price or sell at that price within 30 days.

## 15. Governing Law and Jurisdiction

15.1. This Agreement is governed by the laws of Malta.

15.2. The Malta Arbitration Centre has exclusive jurisdiction for disputes arising from this Agreement.

## 16. Limitation of Liability

16.1. No Shareholder is personally liable for the debts or obligations of the Company beyond the nominal value of their subscribed shares.

16.2. The Company shall maintain adequate D&O insurance at its own expense.

16.3. Neither Shareholder shall be liable to the other for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages arising from this Agreement.

## 17. General

17.1. This Agreement supersedes all prior agreements between the parties regarding the Company.

17.2. Amendments require written agreement signed by both Shareholders.

17.3. If any provision is unenforceable, remaining provisions survive.

17.4. Notices in writing to registered addresses or email addresses on file.
