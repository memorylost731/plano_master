## PART I -- MEMORANDUM OF ASSOCIATION

### 1. Company Name

1.1. The name of the Company is **PlanO Pro Ltd**.

### 2. Registered Office

2.1. The registered office is at [BDO MALTA ADDRESS, Malta].

### 3. Objects

3.1. The Company is established for the purpose of developing, operating, and commercialising software-as-a-service products, with particular focus on floor-plan recognition, renovation estimation, and contractor management tools.

3.2. The Company may carry on any lawful trade or business ancillary to or connected with the above, including holding intellectual property, licensing software, and entering partnerships.

4.1. The liability of each member is limited to the amount, if any, unpaid on the shares held by that member.

## PART II -- ARTICLES OF ASSOCIATION

### 7. Interpretation

7.1. "Act" means Companies Act, Cap 386. "Shareholders Agreement" means the agreement of even date between the Shareholders and the Company.

7.2. In case of conflict between these Articles and the Shareholders Agreement, the Shareholders Agreement prevails to the extent permitted by law.

### 8. Directors

8.1. The Company shall have a minimum of one and maximum of three directors.

8.2. The initial sole director is a nominee appointed by BDO Malta (the "BDO Nominee").

8.3. **Appointment and removal** of directors requires an ordinary resolution of shareholders (simple majority).

8.4. The director shall act in accordance with the Shareholders Agreement and shall not exercise powers on Reserved Matters without the requisite shareholder approval.

8.5. The director is entitled to be indemnified by the Company against liability incurred in the proper performance of duties, to the fullest extent permitted by the Act.

### 9. Powers of Directors

9.1. Subject to the Act, the Articles, and the Shareholders Agreement, the director manages the Company's business.

9.2. **The director shall NOT without shareholder approval:**
- (a) Borrow sums exceeding EUR [25,000];
- (b) Enter contracts with annual value exceeding EUR [25,000];
- (c) Issue or allot shares;
- (d) Declare dividends;
- (e) Commence or settle litigation;
- (f) Appoint key personnel with salary exceeding EUR [50,000];
- (g) Grant security over Company assets;
- (h) Amend these Articles.

### 10. Shareholders Meetings

10.1. The Annual General Meeting shall be held within 15 months of incorporation and subsequently within 6 months of each financial year end.

10.2. Extraordinary General Meetings may be convened by the director or shareholders holding 10% or more of shares, on 14 days' written notice.

10.3. Quorum: shareholders representing more than 50% of shares, present in person or by proxy.

10.4. Resolutions may be passed in writing without a meeting if signed by shareholders holding the requisite majority.

10.5. **Voting thresholds** follow the Shareholders Agreement: ordinary (>50%), supermajority (75%), unanimous (100%) for specified matters.

### 11. Transfer of Shares

11.1. Shares are not freely transferable. All transfers are subject to the restrictions in the Shareholders Agreement (ROFR, tag-along, drag-along, lock-up).

11.2. The director shall not register a transfer that violates these Articles or the Shareholders Agreement.

11.3. No shares shall be offered to the public.

### 12. Dividends

12.1. Dividends may only be declared by shareholder resolution.

12.2. The no-dividend policy in the Shareholders Agreement applies until modified by unanimous shareholder consent.

### 13. Accounts and Audit

13.1. The Company shall keep proper books of account as required by the Act.

13.2. Annual financial statements shall be prepared and audited in accordance with the Act.

13.3. The Shareholders are entitled to inspect Company records at reasonable times.

### 14. Indemnification

14.1. To the fullest extent permitted by the Act, the Company indemnifies every director and officer against all costs, charges, losses, expenses, and liabilities incurred in the proper performance of their duties.

14.2. The Company shall maintain D&O insurance with coverage of not less than EUR [1,000,000].

### 15. Winding-Up

15.1. Voluntary winding-up requires a unanimous shareholder resolution.

15.2. On winding-up, surplus assets are distributed to shareholders pro rata to their shareholding.

### 16. Governing Law

16.1. These Articles are governed by the laws of Malta.
