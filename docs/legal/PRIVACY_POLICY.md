## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how PlanO Pro Ltd ("PlanO", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data when you use our floor plan and renovation platform ("Service").

We are committed to GDPR compliance (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the Malta Data Protection Act (Cap. 586). As a Malta-registered company, our lead supervisory authority is the **Office of the Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC)**.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Account Data
- Email address, display name, password (hashed)
- Billing information (processed by Stripe/BTCPay — we do not store card numbers)
- Subscription tier and usage metrics

### 2.2 User Content
- Floor plans you create, upload, or generate
- Project metadata (names, notes, service selections)
- Uploaded images (floor plan photos, PDFs)

### 2.3 Usage Data
- Pages visited, features used, time spent
- Device type, browser, operating system, screen resolution
- IP address (anonymized after 90 days)
- Referral source

### 2.4 AI Processing Data
- Floor plan detection results (wall positions, room classifications)
- Cost estimation inputs and outputs
- Material and service selections

### 2.5 Cookies
We use:
- **Essential cookies** — session management, authentication (required)
- **Analytics cookies** — anonymous usage statistics (opt-in)
- No advertising or tracking cookies

| Purpose | Legal Basis (GDPR Art. 6) | Data Used |
|---------|--------------------------|-----------|
| Provide the Service | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) | Account, User Content, AI Processing |

### 3.1 AI Training — Derived Data
We extract anonymous statistical features from floor plans (e.g., average room sizes, wall thickness distributions, material preferences) to improve our AI models. This Derived Data:

- **Cannot** identify you, your property, or any individual
- **Does not** contain addresses, GPS coordinates, or personal identifiers
- **Does not** include recognizable floor plan images
- Is processed under **legitimate interest** (Art. 6(1)(f)) with a documented balancing test
- Can be **opted out** of at any time (see Section 7)

| **Access** your data | Account settings or email privacy@plano.hacking.eu |
| **Rectify** inaccurate data | Account settings |

## 8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures:

- All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest
- Authentication via bcrypt-hashed passwords + optional TOTP 2FA
- Infrastructure secured with firewall rules, SSH key-only access
- Regular security audits
- Access controls: minimum necessary access for staff
- Incident response plan with 72-hour breach notification (GDPR Art. 33)
