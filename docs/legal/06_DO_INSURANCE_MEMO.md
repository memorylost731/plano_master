**To:** Board of Directors, PlanO Pro Ltd
**From:** Shareholders

**Re:** Mandatory D&O Insurance Coverage

---

## 1. Purpose

1.1. This memo establishes the Company's obligation to maintain Directors and Officers (D&O) liability insurance, as required by the Shareholders Agreement (Section 16.2) and the Articles of Association (Section 14.2).

## 2. Required Coverage

2.1. **Minimum coverage:** EUR 1,000,000 per claim and in aggregate.

2.2. **Policy type:** Claims-made D&O liability insurance from a reputable insurer authorised in Malta or the EU.

2.3. **Scope of coverage:**

| Coverage Area | Description |
|--------------|-------------|
| **Side A -- Personal liability** | Claims against directors/officers where Company cannot indemnify (e.g., insolvency) |
| **Side B -- Company reimbursement** | Reimburses Company for indemnification payments to directors/officers |
| **Side C -- Entity coverage** | Claims against the Company itself (securities claims, if applicable) |

## 3. What Must Be Covered

3.1. Personal liability of directors and officers for wrongful acts in their capacity.

3.2. Legal defense costs (duty to defend, not just reimburse).

3.3. Regulatory investigation costs.

3.4. Settlement amounts (with insurer consent).

3.5. Employment practices liability (wrongful termination, discrimination claims).

3.6. Coverage for past, present, and future directors and officers.

## 4. What Is Excluded (Standard)

4.1. Fraud, dishonesty, or criminal acts (after final adjudication).

4.2. Personal profit or advantage to which the insured was not legally entitled.

4.3. Prior known claims or circumstances.

4.4. Bodily injury or property damage (covered by general liability).

## 5. Cost and Payment

5.1. **The Company pays** all premiums. This is a Company expense, not a personal expense of any director or shareholder.

5.2. Estimated annual premium: EUR [2,000-5,000] for a startup of this size (to be quoted).

5.3. Premium is a Reserved Matter only if it exceeds EUR [10,000].

## 6. Run-Off Coverage

6.1. The Company must maintain run-off (tail) coverage for a minimum of **6 years** after any director or officer ceases to serve.

6.2. This obligation survives Company sale, merger, or winding-up and must be addressed in any Exit Event documentation.

## 8. Regulatory Note

8.1. While D&O insurance is not strictly mandatory under Malta Companies Act Cap 386, it is **industry best practice** and is contractually required under the Company's Shareholders Agreement and Director Service Agreement.

8.2. Malta's 5% effective corporate tax rate (via the refund system) makes the premium cost negligible relative to the protection it provides.
