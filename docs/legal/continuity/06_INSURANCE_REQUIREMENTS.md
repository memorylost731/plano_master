### 1.3. Professional Indemnity (PI) Insurance

**Purpose:** Covers claims arising from PlanO's estimation services. Critical because clients may treat AI-generated cost estimates as professional advice.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Coverage | EUR [500,000 - 1,000,000] per claim |
| Covers | Errors in cost estimates, incorrect measurements, AI-generated advice |
| Scope | Malta and EU (expand per geographic expansion) |
| Estimated annual premium | EUR [1,000 - 3,000] |

**Why this matters:** The PlanO Blind Spots & Risk document identifies "Estimation Liability Risk" -- clients treating estimates as guarantees. PI insurance is the financial backstop behind the legal disclaimers in the Terms of Service.

### 1.4. Cyber Insurance

**Purpose:** Covers costs of data breach response, regulatory fines (where insurable), business interruption from cyber incidents, and ransomware extortion.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Coverage | EUR [500,000] |
| Covers | Data breach notification costs, forensic investigation, regulatory defense, business interruption, ransomware (excluding ransom payment) |
| Scope | Global (SaaS serves EU customers, data stored on Malta-jurisdiction server) |
| Estimated annual premium | EUR [1,500 - 4,000] |

**Note:** Some GDPR fines are insurable in Malta (regulatory defense costs), but administrative fines themselves may not be insurable depending on the policy and jurisdiction. Confirm with insurer.

### 1.5. Business Interruption Insurance

**Purpose:** Covers lost revenue during extended outage.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Coverage | 6 months of projected revenue |
| Trigger | Extended outage exceeding [48 hours] caused by insured event |
| Estimated annual premium | EUR [500 - 1,500] (low revenue stage = low premium) |

**Practical note:** At current revenue levels (pre-launch), this policy costs very little. Worth binding now so coverage is in place as revenue grows.
