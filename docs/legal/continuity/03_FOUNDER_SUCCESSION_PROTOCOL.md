## 1. Purpose

1.1. This protocol defines what happens to shares, voting rights, and operational control when a founder is permanently or temporarily unable to fulfil their role.

1.2. This document supplements (does not override) the Shareholders Agreement. Where conflict exists, the Shareholders Agreement prevails.

## 2. Permanent Incapacity (Medical)

2.1. **Definition:** A founder is deemed permanently incapacitated if two independent medical practitioners certify in writing that the founder is unable to participate in business decisions for a continuous period exceeding 180 days.

2.2. **Share treatment:** Shares remain the property of the incapacitated founder (or their estate/legal guardian). This is a Good Leaver event per Shareholders Agreement Section 7.1.

2.3. **Voting rights:** The incapacitated founder's legal representative (guardian, attorney under power of attorney, or court-appointed administrator) may exercise voting rights. However:
- For operational efficiency, the surviving active founder receives a **voting proxy** for all matters except Reserved Matters requiring 75% or unanimous consent
- Reserved Matters still require the legal representative's written consent
- [ ] Both founders should execute a durable power of attorney naming a trusted person who can act on their behalf for PlanO-related matters specifically

2.4. **Buyback option:** The surviving founder has the option (not obligation) to purchase the incapacitated founder's vested shares at FMV, exercisable within 12 months of the incapacity determination. Payment may be structured over 24 months.

## 3. Death of a Founder

3.1. **Immediate effect:** Shares pass to the deceased founder's estate per their will (or intestate succession law if no will -- see Document 04).

3.2. **Company right of first refusal:** Before any inherited shares are transferred to a third party, the Company (or surviving founder) has the right to purchase them at FMV. Exercise period: 90 days from date of grant of probate or letters of administration.

3.3. **Voting during probate:** The surviving founder receives a temporary voting proxy for ALL shares (including the deceased's) for operational decisions only. Reserved Matters requiring 75% or unanimous consent are frozen until the estate is settled, unless the executor provides written consent.

3.4. **Business continuity during probate:**
- BDO Nominee continues as director, taking instructions from the surviving founder for ordinary matters
- [ ] RECOMMENDATION: Include in each founder's will a specific clause authorizing the executor to cooperate with the surviving founder and BDO for business continuity (see Document 04)

3.5. **Key person insurance payout:** If key person insurance is in place (see Document 06), the payout goes to the Company and may be used to fund the share buyback at FMV, ensuring the estate receives fair value and the surviving founder obtains full control.

## 4. Voluntary Departure

4.1. **Good Leaver departure:** A founder who departs voluntarily after the cliff period, with 90 days written notice and a proper handover, retains vested shares at FMV. Unvested shares transfer to the remaining founder at nominal value (EUR 1.00/share). Reference: Shareholders Agreement Section 7.1.

4.2. **Bad Leaver departure:** A founder who departs under Bad Leaver conditions (breach, fraud, conviction, pre-cliff departure, competitive activity) forfeits ALL shares at nominal value. Reference: Shareholders Agreement Section 7.2.

4.3. **Handover obligations for any voluntary departure:**
- 90-day notice period
- Complete credential transfer and escrow update
- Written documentation of all undocumented knowledge (see Key Person Risk document)
- Technical handover to replacement or surviving founder
- Removal from all system access upon departure completion

## 5. Divorce or Personal Legal Proceedings

5.1. **Ring-fencing provision:** Shares in PlanO Pro Ltd are business assets. Each founder commits to include in any prenuptial, postnuptial, or separation agreement a clause excluding PlanO shares from the matrimonial estate.

5.2. **If a court orders share transfer to a spouse:** The Company's ROFR (Shareholders Agreement Section 6.2) applies. The surviving founder may purchase the shares at FMV rather than accept a third-party shareholder.

5.3. **Malta law note:** Under the Marriage Act (Cap 255), Malta's default matrimonial regime is community of acquests. Shares acquired during marriage may form part of the community unless excluded by contract. Pre-nuptial or post-nuptial contract is strongly recommended.

5.4. **Bulgarian law note (for Ogi):** Under the Bulgarian Family Code, the default regime is community of property. Shares acquired during marriage may be community property. A matrimonial property contract (brahen dogovor) can exclude specific assets. This must be notarized and registered with the Civil Registry.

## 6. Criminal Proceedings Against a Founder

6.1. **Charge (not conviction):** No automatic effect on shares or voting. However, if the charge relates to fraud, dishonesty, or financial crime, the Board (BDO Nominee + other founder) may suspend the charged founder's operational access (not voting rights) pending resolution.

6.2. **Conviction:** A criminal conviction for fraud, dishonesty, or any offence carrying imprisonment of 12 months or more triggers Bad Leaver status. Reference: Shareholders Agreement Section 7.2.

6.3. **Regulatory disqualification:** If a founder is disqualified from holding a directorship under Malta law (Cap 386 Section 320), this does not directly affect shareholding but triggers a review of their operational role.

## 7. Succession Hierarchy

If both founders are unavailable, operational authority passes in this order:

1. **BDO Nominee Director** -- limited to statutory obligations and maintenance of operations
2. **Designated Emergency Contact** -- as named in Document 05 (Escrow & Dead Man's Switch)
3. **Executor(s) of respective estates** -- for share-related decisions
4. **Malta Arbitration Centre** -- to appoint a temporary administrator if all above fail
