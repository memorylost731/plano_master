# Director Service Agreement

**Between:** PlanO Pro Ltd ("the Company") and [BDO NOMINEE NAME] ("the Director"), nominated by BDO Malta

**Date:** [DATE]

---

## 1. Appointment

1.1. The Company appoints the Director as sole director with effect from [DATE].

1.2. The Director is a nominee of BDO Malta and acts in accordance with the Shareholders Agreement and the lawful instructions of the Shareholders.

## 2. Duties

2.1. The Director shall exercise the duties of a director under the Companies Act, Cap 386, including the duties of care, skill, and diligence.

2.2. The Director shall:
- (a) Maintain the Company's statutory registers and filings with the Malta Business Registry;
- (b) Sign annual returns, financial statements, and tax filings;
- (c) Convene shareholder meetings as required;
- (d) Execute documents on behalf of the Company within authorised limits;
- (e) Ensure compliance with applicable Malta legislation.

## 3. Limitations and Reserved Matters

3.1. **The Director shall NOT without prior written Shareholder approval:**
- Issue, allot, or repurchase shares;
- Declare or pay dividends;
- Borrow or lend sums exceeding EUR [25,000];
- Enter, vary, or terminate contracts exceeding EUR [25,000] annual value;
- Commence, settle, or defend litigation exceeding EUR [25,000];
- Grant security or guarantees over Company assets;
- Appoint or dismiss key employees with salary exceeding EUR [50,000];
- Amend the Memorandum or Articles of Association;
- Open or close bank accounts;
- Enter related-party transactions;
- Change the Company's auditor or registered agent;
- Take any action on the Reserved Matters listed in the Shareholders Agreement.

3.2. The Director shall obtain instructions in writing and maintain a record of all instructions received from Shareholders.

## 4. Fees and Expenses

4.1. The Company pays BDO Malta an annual retainer of EUR [AMOUNT] for nominee director services, payable [quarterly/annually] in advance.

4.2. Reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred in performance of duties are reimbursed on presentation of receipts.

4.3. Fees are subject to review annually by Shareholder agreement.

## 5. Indemnification

5.1. The Company indemnifies the Director against all claims, costs, damages, expenses, and liabilities arising from the proper performance of duties under this Agreement, to the fullest extent permitted by the Act.

5.2. This indemnity does not cover fraud, wilful default, or gross negligence by the Director.

5.3. The indemnity survives termination of this Agreement.

## 6. D&O Insurance

6.1. The Company shall maintain Directors and Officers liability insurance with a reputable insurer, with minimum coverage of EUR [1,000,000], at the Company's expense.

6.2. The policy shall cover: personal liability claims, regulatory investigations, legal defense costs, and settlement amounts.

6.3. Coverage must be maintained for 6 years after the Director ceases to hold office.

## 7. Confidentiality

7.1. The Director shall keep confidential all Company information obtained in the course of duties. This obligation is perpetual.

## 8. Term and Termination

8.1. This Agreement continues until terminated by either party on 30 days' written notice.

8.2. The Shareholders may remove the Director by ordinary resolution at any time, subject to the Director's right to reasonable notice.

8.3. BDO Malta may replace the nominee individual on 30 days' notice, provided the replacement meets the same qualifications.

8.4. On termination, the Director shall return all Company property and records.

## 9. Governing Law

9.1. Governed by the laws of Malta. Disputes resolved by the Malta Arbitration Centre.
