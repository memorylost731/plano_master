## Resolutions

### Resolution 1.1 — Incorporation Matters
IT IS RESOLVED THAT the incorporation of PlanO Pro Ltd as a private limited liability company under the Malta Companies Act (Cap 386) is hereby ratified, with:
- Registered office: [BDO Malta address]
- Authorised share capital: €1,165 divided into 1,000 ordinary shares of €1.165 each
- Issued share capital: 1,000 shares (600 to O. Ignatov, 400 to H. Majoie)
- Financial year end: 31 December
- First financial period: [Incorporation date] to 31 December 2026

### Resolution 1.2 — Appointment of Director
IT IS RESOLVED THAT [BDO Nominee Name], nominated by BDO Malta, is appointed as sole director of the Company, subject to the terms of the Director Service Agreement dated [date].

### Resolution 1.3 — Adoption of Reserved Matters
IT IS RESOLVED THAT the Reserved Matters set out in Schedule 1 of the Shareholders Agreement dated [date] are adopted. The Director shall not take any action listed as a Reserved Matter without prior written approval of shareholders holding at least [75%/100%] of voting shares, as specified per matter.

### Resolution 1.4 — Banking Arrangements
IT IS RESOLVED THAT:
(a) The Company shall open current accounts with a minimum of TWO banking institutions;
(b) [Bank 1: Bank of Valletta] and [Bank 2: HSBC Malta / Revolut Business] are approved;
(c) Online banking shall be enabled on all accounts;
(d) Single signatory authority up to €5,000 per transaction is granted to the Director;
(e) Any transaction exceeding €5,000 requires written shareholder approval;
(f) No credit facilities, overdrafts, or loans shall be taken without unanimous shareholder approval;
(g) The Company shall maintain a minimum cash reserve equal to 6 months' operating costs.

### Resolution 1.5 — Payment Processors
IT IS RESOLVED THAT:
(a) Stripe (stripe.com) is approved as the primary card payment processor;
(b) BTCPay Server (self-hosted) is approved for Bitcoin payment processing;
(c) No other payment processors shall be engaged without shareholder approval;
(d) Payment processor credentials shall be held by the Company, not individuals.

### Resolution 1.6 — Subscription to Services
IT IS RESOLVED THAT the Director is authorised to enter into service agreements with a total commitment not exceeding €500/month without further shareholder approval, including but not limited to: domain registration, hosting, analytics, email services.

### Resolution 1.7 — Intellectual Property Assignment
IT IS RESOLVED THAT the IP Assignment Agreements with each of O. Ignatov and H. Majoie (dated [date]) are ratified, confirming that all intellectual property in the PlanO platform, including software, AI models, training data, designs, and domain names, is owned by the Company.

### Resolution 1.8 — Consultancy Arrangements
IT IS RESOLVED THAT the Founder Consultancy Agreements with each of O. Ignatov and H. Majoie (dated [date]) are approved. Initial compensation: €0 per month, to be reviewed when the Company achieves positive monthly cash flow for 3 consecutive months.

### Resolution 1.9 — Insurance
IT IS RESOLVED THAT the Director shall obtain, within 30 days of incorporation:
(a) Directors & Officers liability insurance (minimum €1,000,000 cover);
(b) Professional indemnity insurance (minimum €500,000 cover);
(c) Cyber insurance (minimum €250,000 cover);
with the Company as policy holder and premium payer.

### Resolution 1.10 — Data Protection
IT IS RESOLVED THAT:
(a) The Company shall register with the Malta Information and Data Protection Commissioner (IDPC) within 30 days of commencing data processing;
(b) H. Majoie is appointed as interim Data Protection Officer until a formal DPO is engaged;
(c) The Privacy Policy dated [date] is adopted as the Company's data protection policy.

### Resolution 1.11 — Holding Company Structure
IT IS RESOLVED THAT the shareholders shall, at the earliest practical date, incorporate PlanO HoldCo Ltd (Malta) and transfer their shares in PlanO Pro Ltd to PlanO HoldCo Ltd, for the purpose of tax-efficient exit structuring under the Malta participation exemption.
