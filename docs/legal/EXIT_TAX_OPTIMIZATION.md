

# PlanO Pro Ltd Exit: Complete Tax & Logistics Analysis

## 1. Malta Capital Gains Tax on Share Sales

**Standard CGT rate**: Malta taxes capital gains on share transfers at **0% for shares in Malta companies** — provided the disposal qualifies under the **participation exemption** (Article 12(1)(u) of the Income Tax Act).

**Participation Exemption Requirements** (all must be met):

- Malta company holds an "equity holding" (meaning at least **10% of equity** OR acquisition cost of at least €1.164M, or held for furtherance of business, with right to appoint a director or 50%+ profits on winding up)
- The holding is in a company that either: (a) is resident/incorporated in the EU, or (b) is subject to foreign tax of at least 15%, or (c) does not derive more than 50% of income from passive interest/royalties — the "anti-abuse" test
- **No minimum holding period** for the exemption to apply to capital gains (the 12-month rule applies only to dividend exemptions)

**Critical point for PlanO**: The participation exemption applies to the **shareholder selling shares in PlanO**, not PlanO itself. For Ogi and Hadrien to use it, they would need to hold PlanO shares **through a Malta holding company** — individual shareholders selling directly are taxed at personal income tax rates (0-35% progressive scale for Malta residents, withholding for non-residents).

**Share deal vs asset deal**: A share deal is strongly preferred. An asset deal would trigger corporate tax in PlanO (35% headline, refundable to effective 5%) on the gain from asset disposal, PLUS personal tax on distribution. Share deal = single layer of tax.

**Malta refund system (6/7ths refund, effective 5%)**: This applies to **dividends and trading income**, NOT to capital gains. The participation exemption is the relevant mechanism for capital gains, and it provides a full 0% — better than the 5% effective rate.

## 3. Pre-Sale Tax Optimization (Legal)

### Malta Holding Company Interposition — THE KEY MOVE

**Structure**: Both shareholders transfer their PlanO shares into a newly formed **Malta holding company (HoldCo)**. HoldCo then sells PlanO to the buyer.

**Result**: HoldCo receives the €16.7M, pays **0% CGT** under the participation exemption. Shareholders then extract funds via dividends (5% effective after 6/7ths refund) or liquidation (potentially 0% under participation exemption if another holding sits above).

**Timeline needed**: The share transfer into HoldCo must be structured carefully:
- No minimum holding period for capital gains exemption
- Transfer of shares into HoldCo should be at **market value** (to avoid future disputes) or at book value with a reorganization relief
- Allow **3-6 months minimum** before closing to establish substance and avoid GAAR challenge
- **Start immediately** — this is the single highest-value optimization

### IP Box Regime

Malta's IP regime provides a **tax rate as low as 1.75%** (via deductions on qualifying IP income). Software and AI algorithms qualify if there is a patent, copyright, or similar IP right. However, this benefits **ongoing royalty income**, not a capital exit. Useful only if you restructure part of the deal as an IP license with ongoing royalties (earn-out disguised as royalty — aggressive, requires careful structuring).

### Retained Earnings

Distribute retained earnings **before** sale as dividends (5% effective rate via refund system). The buyer will otherwise pay for them in the share price, and the shareholders get no additional value — the buyer prices shares net of distributable reserves. Cleaning the balance sheet also simplifies working capital negotiations.

### Earn-Out Tax Treatment

Earn-out payments are taxed when received (not when contingent). For participation exemption purposes, each earn-out payment is treated as additional disposal proceeds — so 0% applies if the exemption conditions were met at the time of disposal. **Clean exit is simpler and more tax-efficient than earn-out.**

### Deferred Consideration

Taxed when it becomes unconditionally receivable. If held through HoldCo with participation exemption, 0% regardless of timing.
