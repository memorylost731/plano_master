## Key Patterns and Competitive Intelligence

### Content Ownership Spectrum (Worst to Best)
1. **Planner5D**: Full irrevocable assignment. Users lose all rights. **Avoid this.**
2. **Floorplanner (free)**: Perpetual transferable sublicensable license. Aggressive.
3. **MagicPlan/Houzz**: Perpetual irrevocable license, user retains ownership nominally.
4. **RoomSketcher**: Unrestricted irrevocable license. Aggressive.
5. **Floorplanner (paid)**: Customer owns exclusively. **Good model.**
6. **Canva/Figma**: User owns, platform gets operational license only. **Best practice.**

### AI Training Approaches
1. **No clause** (Floorplanner, MagicPlan, Planner5D): Legal risk, ambiguity.
2. **Explicit prohibition on third parties** (Houzz): Protects users from external scraping.
3. **Consent-based opt-in** (RoomSketcher): Prior consent required.
4. **User-controlled toggle** (Canva, Figma): Best practice, but Figma got sued anyway.

### Liability Caps
- Lowest: Houzz ($100 or 12-month fees)
- Standard: 12-month fees (MagicPlan, Planner5D, Trimble)
- Highest: Floorplanner (6-month fees, max EUR 5,000)

### Dispute Resolution
- **Courts**: Floorplanner (Rotterdam), RoomSketcher (Oslo), Planner5D (Vilnius for <EUR 50K)
- **Arbitration**: Houzz (ADR Services), MagicPlan, Planner5D (>EUR 50K)
- **EU-first recommendation for PlanO**: Courts of Malta, Maltese/EU law

### Refund Policies
- **Industry standard**: Non-refundable across the board.
- **MagicPlan**: 50% early termination penalty. Most aggressive.
- **EU requirement**: 14-day cooling-off period for consumers (mandatory).

## Recommendations for PlanO

### Must Have (Legal Compliance)
1. **GDPR Article 13/14 compliant privacy policy** with legal basis, DPO, data subject rights
2. **14-day EU cooling-off period** (Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU)
3. **"Cancel contract" button** (Directive 2023/2673, enforceable June 2026)
4. **Clear user content ownership** - follow Canva model
5. **Explicit AI training opt-out** - follow Canva model with toggle
6. **Construction accuracy disclaimer** - unique to PlanO's domain

### Differentiators (Competitive Advantage)
1. **Plain English terms** (most competitors use dense legalese)
2. **User owns everything** (differentiates from Planner5D's full assignment)
3. **Transparent AI policy** with opt-out (avoids Figma's class action scenario)
4. **Pro-rata refund on annual plans** (unique, builds trust)
5. **30-day data export after cancellation** (most delete immediately)
6. **Renovation-specific disclaimers** (no competitor addresses this)

### Risk Mitigation
1. **Marketplace liability**: PlanO connects clients with contractors - need intermediary protections
2. **Estimate accuracy**: Disclaimers that estimates are indicative, not guaranteed
3. **Professional advice**: Floor plans are not architectural drawings, not substitutes for licensed architects
4. **Construction outcomes**: Zero liability for actual construction results
5. **Vetted Provider disclaimer**: Vetting does not guarantee quality of work
