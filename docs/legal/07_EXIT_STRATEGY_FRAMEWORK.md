## 1. Exit Objective

1.1. Sell 100% of PlanO Pro Ltd to a SaaS acquisition fund or strategic acquirer at a minimum company valuation of **EUR 16,700,000**, yielding EUR 10,020,000 for Shareholder A (60%) and EUR 6,680,000 for Shareholder B (40%).

1.2. **Clean exit:** No personal guarantees, no earnouts tied to founder performance, no non-competes beyond 24 months.

## 2. Valuation Methodology

2.1. SaaS companies are valued on Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) multiples. For a profitable, growing B2B SaaS in the EU:

| Metric | Conservative (5x) | Mid-range (7.5x) | Optimistic (10x) |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Required ARR** | EUR 3,340,000 | EUR 2,227,000 | EUR 1,670,000 |
| **Required MRR** | EUR 278,000 | EUR 186,000 | EUR 139,000 |
| **Users at EUR 10 ARPU** | 27,833 | 18,556 | 13,917 |
| **Users at EUR 15 ARPU** | 18,556 | 12,370 | 9,278 |

2.2. **Valuation boosters** (command higher multiples):
- Net Revenue Retention >110%
- Gross margin >80% (SaaS standard, PlanO infrastructure costs are near-zero)
- YoY growth >50%
- Low churn (<5% monthly)
- AI/ML differentiation (Rasta engine, 15 AI agents)
- Proprietary training data

2.3. **Valuation method for internal triggers:** Independent valuation by a Big Four firm or equivalent, using a blended approach (DCF + ARR multiple + comparable transactions).

## 5. Exit Readiness Checklist

### 5.1. Financial
- [ ] 3 years audited financial statements (Malta statutory audit)
- [ ] Monthly MRR/ARR tracking with cohort analysis
- [ ] Unit economics documented (LTV, CAC, payback period, gross margin)
- [ ] Clean cap table, no convertible instruments, no debt
- [ ] Malta 5% effective tax rate properly structured (holding company refund)
- [ ] Transfer pricing documentation (if applicable)

### 5.2. Legal
- [ ] Shareholders Agreement executed and current
- [ ] All IP properly assigned to Company (IP Assignment Agreement)
- [ ] No outstanding litigation or regulatory issues
- [ ] GDPR/data protection compliance documentation
- [ ] All material contracts catalogued with renewal dates
- [ ] No personal guarantees by shareholders
- [ ] D&O insurance current with run-off provision
- [ ] Clean corporate minute book maintained by BDO

### 5.3. Technical
- [ ] Codebase documented and maintainable without founders
- [ ] Infrastructure reproducible (Docker, CI/CD)
- [ ] Security audit completed (penetration test, SOC 2 Type II or equivalent)
- [ ] No dependency on founder personal infrastructure (migrate off hacking.eu)
- [ ] SLA and uptime history documented (99.9%+ target)
- [ ] Data architecture documented, GDPR-compliant data deletion capability

### 5.4. Commercial
- [ ] Customer contracts on standard terms (no bespoke deals)
- [ ] Diversified customer base (no client >10% of revenue)
- [ ] Documented sales process and pipeline
- [ ] Brand assets (trademark registrations, domains)
- [ ] NPS score >40

### 5.5. Team
- [ ] Key-person risk mitigated (operations manual, documented processes)
- [ ] BDO nominee director facilitates clean management transition
- [ ] Consultant agreements terminable on 90 days' notice
- [ ] No ongoing founder obligations post-close (except 24-month non-compete)

## 6. Data Room Contents

The following must be prepared and maintained in a virtual data room from Month 30 onwards:

1. **Corporate:** Certificate of incorporation, Articles, Shareholders Agreement, board minutes, cap table
2. **Financial:** Audited accounts (3 years), management accounts (monthly), tax returns, bank statements
3. **Legal:** All material contracts, IP assignment, consultant agreements, insurance policies
4. **Commercial:** Customer list (anonymised), revenue by cohort, churn analysis, pipeline
5. **Technical:** Architecture documentation, security audit, uptime reports, technology stack overview
6. **HR:** Consultant agreements, key personnel, organisational chart
7. **Compliance:** GDPR documentation, privacy policy, terms of service, regulatory filings
8. **Tax:** Malta holding structure documentation, refund applications, transfer pricing

## 7. Transaction Structure Preferences

7.1. **Preferred:** Share sale (100% of shares). Shareholders receive cash at closing. Clean break.

7.2. **Acceptable:** Asset sale, provided shareholders receive equivalent value after tax.

7.3. **Avoid:**
- Earnouts tied to founder performance (acceptable only if <10% of total consideration and time-limited to 12 months)
- Personal guarantees or warranties extending beyond 18 months
- Escrow exceeding 10% of consideration
- Non-competes beyond 24 months or outside the EU

7.4. **Malta tax advantage:** Share sale by non-Malta-resident individuals may be exempt from Malta capital gains tax (subject to proper structuring and advice from BDO).

## 8. Liability Protection at Exit

8.1. **Warranty and Indemnity (W&I) insurance:** The Company (or acquirer) should obtain W&I insurance to cap shareholder warranty exposure.

8.2. **Warranty cap:** Total warranty liability capped at EUR 1 (de minimis) where W&I insurance is obtained, or 10% of consideration otherwise.

8.3. **Warranty period:** Maximum 18 months from closing (24 months for tax warranties).

8.4. **Basket/threshold:** Claims below EUR [50,000] individual / EUR [100,000] aggregate are excluded.

8.5. **No personal guarantees** from shareholders for any Company representations.
