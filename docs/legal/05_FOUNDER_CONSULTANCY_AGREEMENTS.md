# Founder Consultancy Agreement

**Date:** [DATE]

## 1. Appointment

1.1. The Company engages the Consultant as an independent contractor (not an employee) with effect from [DATE].

1.2. **Consultant A (Ognyan Ignatov):** Product strategy, business development, market positioning, client relations, and pricing.

1.3. **Consultant B (Hadrien Majoie):** Technology architecture, infrastructure, AI/ML development, security, and DevOps.

## 2. Independent Contractor Status

2.1. The Consultant is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship, partnership, joint venture, or agency.

2.2. The Consultant is responsible for their own tax obligations, social insurance contributions, and professional insurance.

2.3. The Company shall not withhold tax or make social security contributions on the Consultant's behalf.

2.4. **Liability shield:** The Consultant's liability under this Agreement is limited to the fees received in the 12 months preceding any claim. The Consultant shall not be liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages.

## 3. Compensation

3.1. **Phase 1 (Pre-revenue):** EUR 0 cash compensation. The Consultant's consideration is their equity holding in the Company per the Shareholders Agreement.

3.2. **Phase 2 (Post-profitability):** Monthly consultancy fee of EUR [AMOUNT], commencing when the Company achieves [3 consecutive months of net profitability / EUR X MRR], as determined by the Shareholders.

3.3. **Expenses:** Reasonable and documented business expenses are reimbursed within 30 days of submission, subject to Company approval for expenses exceeding EUR [500].

## 4. Time Commitment

4.1. The Consultant commits a minimum of [20/40] hours per week to Company matters.

4.2. The Consultant manages their own schedule and working arrangements.

## 5. Intellectual Property

5.1. All IP created by the Consultant in the course of performing services under this Agreement belongs to the Company, as set out in the IP Assignment Agreement executed contemporaneously.

5.2. The Consultant shall not use Company IP for personal purposes or for third parties.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1. The Consultant shall not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written Company consent.

6.2. "Confidential Information" includes all business plans, financial data, client lists, technical know-how, source code, AI models, training data, and any information marked or reasonably understood to be confidential.

6.3. This obligation is perpetual and survives termination.

6.4. Exclusions: information that is publicly available, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law.

## 7. Non-Compete

7.1. During the term and for 24 months after termination, the Consultant shall not directly or indirectly:
- (a) Engage in a business competing with the Company within the European Union;
- (b) Solicit Company clients, contractors, or consultants;
- (c) Divert business opportunities from the Company.

7.2. "Competing business" means floor-plan SaaS, renovation estimation, or contractor management software.

## 8. Term and Termination

8.1. This Agreement continues until terminated by either party on 90 days' written notice.

8.2. **Immediate termination** by the Company for: material breach (uncured after 14 days' notice), fraud, criminal conviction, or Bad Leaver events under the Shareholders Agreement.

8.3. On termination, the Consultant shall return all Company property, data, and materials.

8.4. Sections 5 (IP), 6 (Confidentiality), and 7 (Non-Compete) survive termination.

## 9. Liability and Indemnification

9.1. The Consultant's aggregate liability is capped at fees received in the preceding 12 months.

9.2. The Company indemnifies the Consultant against third-party claims arising from the proper performance of services, except where caused by the Consultant's gross negligence or wilful misconduct.

## 10. Governing Law

10.1. Governed by the laws of Malta. Disputes per the Shareholders Agreement (mediation then arbitration).
