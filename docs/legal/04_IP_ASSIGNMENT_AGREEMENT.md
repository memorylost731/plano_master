# Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement

**Between:**

1. **Ognyan Ignatov** ("Assignor A")
2. **Hadrien Majoie** ("Assignor B")

(together, the "Assignors")

**And:** PlanO Pro Ltd, Company No. [C-XXXXX] ("the Company")

**Date:** [DATE]

---

## 1. Background

1.1. The Assignors have developed intellectual property relating to the PlanO floor-plan SaaS platform prior to and in connection with the formation of the Company.

1.2. The Assignors wish to assign all such IP to the Company in exchange for shares in the Company.

## 2. Definitions

2.1. "Assigned IP" means all intellectual property rights described in Section 3, whether registered or unregistered, in all jurisdictions worldwide.

2.2. "Business IP" means any intellectual property created by either Assignor in connection with or relating to the Business after the date of this Agreement.

## 3. Assignment of Existing IP

3.1. Each Assignor hereby irrevocably assigns to the Company, with full title guarantee, all right, title, and interest in:

- (a) **Software:** All source code, object code, algorithms, APIs, databases, and documentation relating to PlanO, including but not limited to: the React frontend, React-Planner integration, Rasta floor-plan detection engine, AI agent system, SaaS dashboard, and all deployment configurations;
- (b) **AI Models and Training Data:** All machine learning models, trained weights, training datasets, evaluation datasets, and model architectures developed for PlanO;
- (c) **Designs:** All UI/UX designs, wireframes, mockups, icons, and visual assets;
- (d) **Domain Names:** plano.mt, [OTHER DOMAINS] and all related DNS configurations;
- (e) **Trademarks:** The PlanO name, logo, and all associated branding;
- (f) **Trade Secrets:** All proprietary methodologies, business processes, algorithms, and know-how;
- (g) **Content:** All marketing materials, documentation, and written content.

3.2. The assignment is **worldwide, perpetual, and irrevocable**.

## 4. Consideration

4.1. The consideration for this assignment is the allotment to each Assignor of their respective shares in the Company as set out in the Shareholders Agreement (720 shares to Assignor A, 480 shares to Assignor B).

4.2. Each Assignor acknowledges the consideration is adequate.

## 5. Future IP

5.1. Each Assignor agrees that all Business IP created after the date of this Agreement **automatically vests in the Company** upon creation.

5.2. To the extent any future Business IP does not automatically vest, each Assignor hereby assigns it to the Company and agrees to execute any documents necessary to perfect the assignment.

5.3. Each Assignor shall promptly disclose to the Company any Business IP they create.

## 6. Moral Rights

6.1. To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, each Assignor waives all moral rights in the Assigned IP and future Business IP, including rights of attribution and integrity.

6.2. Where moral rights cannot be waived, each Assignor consents to any act that would otherwise infringe such rights.

## 7. Further Assurance

7.1. Each Assignor shall, at the Company's expense, execute all documents and take all actions reasonably necessary to:
- (a) Perfect the assignments made under this Agreement;
- (b) Register the Company's ownership of any registrable rights;
- (c) Assist in the prosecution, defense, or enforcement of any assigned rights.

7.2. Each Assignor irrevocably appoints the Company as their attorney to execute documents under this section if the Assignor fails to do so within 14 days of request.

## 8. Warranties

8.1. Each Assignor warrants that:
- (a) They are the sole owner of the IP they assign, or have the right to assign it;
- (b) The IP is free from encumbrances, liens, and third-party claims;
- (c) They have not previously assigned, licensed, or encumbered the IP;
- (d) To their knowledge, the IP does not infringe third-party rights;
- (e) They have not used open-source components in a manner that would impose copyleft obligations on the Company's proprietary code.

## 9. Exclusions

9.1. This Agreement does not assign personal IP unrelated to the Business created outside business hours and without Company resources. Each Assignor shall declare any such IP in writing.

## 10. Governing Law

10.1. Governed by the laws of Malta.
