# PlanO — Simple Explanation

## What is PlanO?

PlanO is a website where people plan their home renovation.

**How it works:**
1. You take a photo of your floor plan (or draw one)
2. Our AI reads the photo and finds all the walls, rooms, and doors
3. You pick what work you want done (new bathroom, paint walls, new floors)
4. PlanO tells you how much it will cost
5. You can see your apartment in 3D
6. We connect you with local builders

**Think of it like:** Google Maps but for the inside of your apartment, with a price tag.

---

## How does PlanO make money?

People pay a monthly subscription:

| Plan | Price | Who it's for |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Free | €0 | Try it once |
| Starter | €9/month | Home renovators |
| Pro | €19/month | Professional builders |
| Agency | €49/month | Building companies |
| One-time | €5/plan | Quick estimate |

**Monthly costs to run PlanO:** About €215.
**Break even:** We need 21 paying customers.
**Profit margin:** 78% — for every €10 a customer pays, we keep €7.85.

## Banking & Money Safety

- Money is in TWO different banks (if one has problems, the other works)
- Card payments through Stripe (trusted by millions of businesses)
- Bitcoin payments through our own server (no middleman)
- No loans, no debt — we only spend what we earn
- 6 months of expenses always saved as emergency fund
- Nobody can spend more than €5,000 without both owners agreeing

## What happens if someone wants to buy PlanO?
